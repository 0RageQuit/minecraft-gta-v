Minecraft × GTA V — 0.1.7 combat update

Crossbow fireworks destroy GTA vehicles within 6 metres of their burst, including direct hits on long vehicles. Timed bursts and Minecraft block impacts also reach GTA. Elytra boosts remain unchanged.
Player bow/crossbow arrows hit Minecraft NPC stand-ins and forward their vanilla arrow damage to GTA at 12 GTA health per Minecraft damage point. NPC stand-ins are maintained even without Minecraft mobs nearby. Minecraft continues to resolve arrows against its own mobs. Projectile hits are consumed once until Minecraft removes them.
TNT destroys GTA vehicles within 8 metres of the blast (twice the Minecraft explosion radius, capped at 16 metres for larger TNT explosions).

Both halves compile. Native-mocked regression checks pass for vehicle radius, direct rocket impacts, NPC arrow damage, Minecraft mob isolation, duplicate hit suppression and empty-list cleanup. The firework injection target was checked against the Minecraft 26.3 class. An initial live test exposed two missing vehicle/ped enumeration native mappings; this corrected package now generates and checks mappings from all native wrapper sources. Live retesting remains necessary.

Install as an update through the existing Melty package workflow using the included melty.json. For an existing manual installation, close both games and replace gta/MCPassthrough.asi beside GTA5.exe, gta/MCPassthrough/native-map.csv in its matching folder, and the passthrough mod jar in the dedicated Prism instance's .minecraft/mods folder. Remove the previous passthrough jar when installing the new one. Keep Fabric API and player worlds/accounts.
