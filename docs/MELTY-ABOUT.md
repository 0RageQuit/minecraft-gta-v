Build with Minecraft blocks, explore Los Santos as your Minecraft character, and soar between skyscrapers with an elytra. Minecraft x GTA V brings real Minecraft Java gameplay into GTA V Legacy’s Story Mode, with both games running together on your PC.

Keep your Minecraft skin, hotbar, inventory and first- or third-person view. Walk, sprint, sneak, jump, fly in Creative mode, or boost your elytra with fireworks. Place and break Minecraft blocks, with placed blocks gaining collision in GTA, and keep your builds in a dedicated Minecraft world. Press **F8** to switch to GTA controls for driving, **E** for your inventory, **F5** to change perspective, **F7** to toggle the Minecraft view, and **F9** to re-level the ground. Offhand items, chat and commands are supported too.

Experimental features include Minecraft mobs interacting with GTA characters and police, cross-game combat and damage, TNT and creeper explosions, bows, crossbows, melee attacks and ender pearls. Survival and Nether-themed portal, mob, fire and lava interactions are still being developed. Lighting, depth, camera and terrain collision help the two worlds fit together; unusual surfaces and moving platforms can still behave unexpectedly. GTA’s existing roads and buildings cannot be mined.

**You need your own GTA V Legacy 1.0.3889.0 and Minecraft Java Edition 26.3 entitlement on Windows.** This release is single player and supports Story Mode. GTA Enhanced and GTA Online are unsupported. Melty installs the mod files and starts both games; no manual mod copying is required. First setup asks you to sign in to your own Minecraft account. Your games supply their own content, and running both together can be demanding on your PC.

Based on Rehan’s universal-modder project, with Minecraft Ring adaptations by siddoff and justbustin.
